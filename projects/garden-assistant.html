01 / OVERVIEW

Turning plant needs into automatic responses.

The project combines a user-facing display module with a separate sensor/control module. The user selects plant sunlight requirements and temperature thresholds using a 4×4 keypad and OLED interface. A second ESP32 monitors sunlight, temperature and soil moisture, and activates appropriate responses such as a supplemental light indicator, a cooling fan or watering pump.

The two ESP32 boards exchange settings and status over ESP-NOW, allowing the user interface to be separated from the plant hardware.

02 / MY WORK

The display module and user experience.

I designed and wired the display-module breadboard, mapped the 4×4 keypad, researched and integrated the OLED libraries, and wrote the user-interface code. The interface guides users through plant preset selection, sunlight settings and temperature thresholds, then displays prompts and status information.

I also worked on the sunlight sensor's threshold logic and contributed to integration testing. My teammates developed the ESP-NOW communication layer and the moisture, watering, temperature and fan subsystems.

04 / TESTING

Integration was the real test.

To validate the complete system, the team tested sunlight thresholds, moisture-triggered watering, temperature-triggered cooling, and user-interface status updates. The final report describes the controls responding as intended during testing.

Limitation and lesson learned

Extended pump operation affected temperature readings and could produce spikes of roughly 10°F. This highlighted the need to consider power distribution, sensor placement and electrical interference when integrating physical subsystems, beyond testing each circuit independently.

Contribution boundaries: I developed the display/UI hardware and code, keypad mapping, OLED integration, and contributed to sunlight logic and testing. Other teammates owned ESP-NOW communications, the moisture/pump subsystem, and the temperature/fan subsystem. This was a team project rather than a solo build.
